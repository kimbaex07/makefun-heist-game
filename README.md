Velvet Vault — a top-down stealth heist game. Slip through three rooms of The Gilded Palm, dodge patrolling guards and sweeping cameras, grab the loot, and get out before the alarm hits 100. Tools at a cost: EMP bursts and noisemakers, two of each per room.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Move with WASD or click-to-move, hold Shift to sneak, E to grab the Sunburst Tiara, Q for EMP, F for noisemaker, P to pause, R to restart after being caught.
